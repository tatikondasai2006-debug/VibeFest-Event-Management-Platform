About {{ settings.festival_name }}

{{ settings.festival_name }} is a student-run techno-cultural festival. It exists to give every student a stage — whether you code, dance, game, paint or simply soak in the vibe. Events are open to enrolled students and staff; each registration is confirmed instantly with a unique reference.

{# ---------- FAQs ---------- #}

Frequently asked questions

How do I register for an event?
Open any published event, click “Register”, fill in your details and consent to the privacy notice. You will receive a unique confirmation reference immediately.
Can I register with the same email twice?
No. Each email address can register once per event. If you need to change your details, contact the event organisers.
What happens when an event is full?
When all {{ 'capacity units' }} are taken, registration closes automatically. Seat counts on every event page come straight from the database, so what you see is accurate.
Is there a registration fee?
Any fees, if applicable, are shown on the individual event page as informational text only. This site does not process payments.
How is my data used?
We store only what is needed to run the event: your name, email, phone and college details. Data is never shown publicly and is available only to event administrators.
